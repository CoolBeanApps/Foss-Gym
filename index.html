Choose whether ranks describe practical gym strength or progress toward an official record.

Bodyweight-relative training bands drive progression.

Which exercises have official records?

Foss Gym uses the absolute men's or women's IPF Open Classic records for competition squat, bench press, and deadlift. It also uses directly comparable untimed Guinness records for consecutive pull-ups, chin-ups, and bodyweight squats when a record exists for the selected comparison profile.

World Class is always the final target and only appears when a published comparable metric exists. Timed feats, machine claims, and exercises without universal rules stay on the normal training scale instead of receiving a made-up record.

Read me: how weighted scores work

Actual 1RM only counts a weighted set when reps equals 1. Other sets are still saved, but they do not change your high score or rank.

Heaviest set counts the largest weight you saved, no matter how many reps you completed.

Estimated 1RM uses your saved weight and reps with the Epley formula. It is an estimate, not a tested one-rep max.

Every mode uses the same saved set history. Changing modes never deletes or rewrites your data.

Weighted rank bands are bodyweight-relative community references. Dumbbell exercises marked "total" use both dumbbells together. Machine brands and lever systems differ, so keep the same setup when comparing your own progress.

BODY INDICATORS

What the numbers mean

BMI

BMI uses weight divided by height squared. Foss Gym shows the standard adult categories: underweight, healthy weight, overweight, and obesity classes. It is a screening indicator, not a diagnosis, and it can misrepresent very muscular people.

Tape body-fat estimate

Use a flexible tape while standing relaxed. Measure height without shoes. Measure the neck just below the Adam's apple. For the male comparison profile, measure the waist level with the navel. For the female comparison profile, measure the natural waist and the widest part of the hips.

Keep the tape level and snug without compressing the skin. Enter waist, neck, height, and hips when requested. Foss Gym calculates an estimate automatically. A manual body-fat entry overrides it.

This is useful for tracking a trend, but it is not as accurate as a professional body-composition assessment.

    Your rank is an estimate.

    Progression style

    Your selected progression style represents the same underlying strength score.

    XP is the universal score underneath every progression style. The scale runs from 0 to 9,900 XP. You can rename it in Settings.

    Weighted movements use the tracking mode selected in Settings. Bodyweight movements use your best clean rep count. Neither is a live leaderboard.

    Equipment, range of motion, technique, and exercise setup can change the result. Compare your own progress using the same form each time.

    A NOTE FROM THE DEV

    Why I made Foss Gym

    Dear user,

    Thank you for installing Foss Gym. It genuinely means a lot to me.

    I made this app because I was frustrated and disappointed with the fitness apps that were already available, including many of the paid ones.

    Too many apps spy on you. They collect your data. They give you 30 days of great features, then take them away and ask you to pay a subscription. They are often cluttered, unintuitive, and built around keeping you subscribed instead of simply serving you.

    I wanted something different.

    I genuinely wanted to lose weight, and I wanted an app that was as simple as possible while still giving me the features I actually needed. No unnecessary complexity. No tracking everything you do. No locking useful features behind a monthly subscription.

    So I decided to make that app myself.

    Foss Gym is meant to be feature-rich, simple to use, and available without requiring a subscription. All I ask is that, if you find it useful and you are able to, you consider making a donation.

    I know building and maintaining something like this is a lot to take on, but I decided it was important enough to do anyway.

    I hope you enjoy the app. If you have questions, feedback, feature requests, or something you think could be improved, please let me know. I will do my best to consider it for a future update.

    If Foss Gym has helped you, please consider donating. It means a lot to me personally, and it gives me the ability to spend more time improving the app and making it better for everyone.

    Finally, shout out to Louis Rossmann and Fulu. Y'all are a major inspiration for this project. I hope more software moves in this direction in the future: built to serve the people using it.

    Thank you for giving Foss Gym a chance.

    Christian
    Developer of Foss Gym
